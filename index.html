About Me

Hi there👋! My name is Manan, and I recently graduated with a Masters of Science in Computer Science from George Mason University in May 2023. I love working with individuals with diverse backgrounds and am very passionate about technology. I am currently looking for Full-time opportunities in Software Development, Full Stack Development, Front-End/Back-End Development, and UI/UX designer roles starting immediately. During my Master's Degree, I worked at Nokia as a Test Automation Development Co-op. My previous work experience includes working as a Full Stack Developer @ Let it Wag, an app that allows animal lovers to connect in real-time with local volunteers, animal feeders, shelters, veterinarians, and so on. Formerly, I lead an Indian Graduate Student Organization as Web Master at George Mason University by creating and managing the website and assisting with all the technical aspects.


Make an impact. Be human.
